2. ROBOTIC / AUTOMATED CNC CUTTING STEP-BY-STEP SEQUENCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[PROGRAM HEADER]
  - Workpiece Zero: Stock End Face (X=0.000, Y=0.000, Z=0.000)
  - Cutting Medium: Fiber Tube Laser or Automated Cold Saw + Drill Station
  - Material Family: Aluminum (Shielding Gas: Nitrogen / Compressed Air)

--- JOB STEP 01: TOP HANDRAIL CAP (ITEM 01) ---
  Step 1.1: Load 3" x 2" x 0.125" Rectangular Aluminum Tube into chuck.
  Step 1.2: Run optical seam-search to align 3.0" face to top Z-plane.
  Step 1.3: Cut raw stock lead-in edge at 90.0° (X=0.000").
  Step 1.4: Execute hole pattern on top 3.0" face (Centerline Y=1.500"):
            * Drill 9x through-holes @ 13/32" (0.406") diameter.
            * Drill spacing: First hole @ X=9.000" from lead end, then repeat
              every 12.000" On-Center (O.C.) along the X-axis.
            * Form 82° countersink tool-pass on each hole to 0.207" depth.
  Step 1.5: Advance tube to X=105.000" and perform 90.0° straight cut-off.
  Step 1.6: Robotic arm unloads Item 01 to Rack A (Handrail Cap).

--- JOB STEP 02: UPRIGHT POSTS (ITEM 02) ---
  Step 2.1: Load 3" x 3" x 0.125" Square Aluminum Tube into chuck.
  Step 2.2: Perform lead-in trim cut at 90.0° (X=0.000").
  Step 2.3: Loop Count = 3 Cycles:
            a. Feed stock to X=40.000".
            b. Perform 90.0° straight cut-off.
            c. Robotic arm unloads component to Rack B (Posts).

--- JOB STEP 03: VERTICAL PICKETS (ITEM 03) ---
  Step 3.1: Load 1.5" x 1.5" x 0.125" Square Aluminum Tube into chuck.
  Step 3.2: Perform lead-in trim cut at 90.0° (X=0.000").
  Step 3.3: Loop Count = 6 Cycles:
            a. Feed stock to X=34.000".
            b. Perform 90.0° straight cut-off.
            c. Robotic arm unloads component to Rack C (Pickets).

--- JOB STEP 04: BASE PLATES (ITEM 04) ---
  Step 4.1: Load 3/8" x 8.0" 6061-T6 Flat Bar / Sheet Stock into CNC bed.
  Step 4.2: Piercing Pass: Pierce 4x 0.500" (1/2") Anchor Bolt Clearance Holes 
            at coordinates (1.0", 1.0"), (7.0", 1.0"), (1.0", 7.0"), (7.0", 7.0").
  Step 4.3: Cut outer perimeter profile 8.000" x 8.000" square.
  Step 4.4: Repeat 3 Cycles. Unload to Rack D (Base Plates).
